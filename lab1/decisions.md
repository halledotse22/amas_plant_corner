Diagnosis – Ama’s Plant Corner
1.	The page is displayed as a zoomed-out desktop version on mobile phones. This is because there is no viewport meta tag within the HTML code. The <head> element/section has no <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0">. This affects almost all of Ama’s customers, since they usually use their phones to browse the site. This makes the website harder to read and navigate. 

2.	The website is difficult to read on mobile devices. The site layout cannot adapt because it was built in fixed pixels. This is because the current layout was designed around fixed desktop dimensions rather than adapting to smaller screens. In the stylesheet, #header, #banner, #content and #footer all have a fixed width of 900px. Additionally, #main and #sidebar have a width of 610px and 240px respectively. There are no responsive media queries in the stylesheet. This affects mobile phone users. 

3.	The “Get in Touch” section is misaligned with the other sections. This is because the contact section is outside the main content container in the HTML, specifically #contact appears outside #content, which has its own width, padding and margin rules in the stylesheet. This affects visitors that want to contact Ama. It makes the website look inconsistent and not professionally maintained. 

4.	Under the plant care tips section, it says that users should click on a topic below to read the care guide but all the tips are displayed after that statement and there are no clickable links in that section. The triggers are plain <div class="guide-trigger"> elements and not buttons. 

5.	Font size, and colour have no uniformity and cohesion. This is because they were added one at a time instead of having a system. For font size, seven sizes (11,13,14,16,18,22 and 31px) were used and were used irregularly. For example, the navigation links (18px) are bigger than the section headings (16px), which are barely bigger than the body (14px). When it comes to colour, the blue, green and orange colours are competing for attention since they ar e all bright colours. Customers could miss important details on the site and could be put off by how inconsistent the site looks.
