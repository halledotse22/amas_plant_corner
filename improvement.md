### The additional improvement: Chat with Ama on WhatsApp

**What it is.** A "Chat with Ama on WhatsApp" button at the top of the Ask Ama section. It opens a chat with her with a greeting already typed.

**Why I added it.** Ama's customers are on their phones and many are first-time plant owners. A message is lower-pressure than a call, and they can send a photo of their plant, which is easier than describing a problem in words.
