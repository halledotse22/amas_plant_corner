### The additional improvement: Chat with Ama on WhatsApp

**What it is.** A "Chat with Ama on WhatsApp" button at the top of the Ask Ama section. It opens a chat with her with a greeting already typed.

**Why I added it.** Ama's customers are on their phones and many are first-time plant owners. A message is lower-pressure than a call, and they can send a photo of their plant, which is easier than describing a problem in words. It also covers the page's biggest gap: the form cannot send anything yet, so WhatsApp is the written route that works today.

**How it holds up against the criteria**
- Semantic HTML: it is a link, not a button, because it takes the visitor somewhere.
- Accessibility: it is 48px tall, its text contrast is 7.4:1, it has a visible focus outline, and its label says exactly what it does.
- Visual consistency: it uses the same moss green button style as Call, with no WhatsApp green, so the page stays calm.
